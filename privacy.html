Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Aran Therapy Center

1. Commitment to Client Confidentiality

At Aran Therapy Center (@aramntherapy), your privacy and personal confidentiality are fundamental to our practice. We adhere strictly to ethical healthcare standards and data protection protocols to ensure that all information shared during therapy booking, consultation, and bodywork sessions remains strictly private.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or reserve an appointment, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact Identification: Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
  • Appointment Preferences: Selected therapy type (Deep Tissue, Swedish Relaxation, Somatic Recovery), date, time, and session format.
  • Health & Body Notes: Voluntary notes regarding specific muscle tension or therapy goals provided during booking.
  • Technical Data: Anonymous website traffic data, IP addresses, and browser information for site optimization.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is collected solely for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • To schedule and confirm your body therapy appointments at Aran Therapy Center.
  • To send appointment confirmation notices, reminders, and updates via email or SMS.
  • To tailor your therapy session according to your specific bodywork preferences.
  • To respond to your inquiries submitted via our online contact form.

We NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers or marketers under any circumstances.

4. Confidentiality & Security Standards

All electronic booking data is transmitted using encrypted Secure Socket Layer (SSL) protocols. Physical intake records and therapy notes are stored securely in password-protected databases accessible exclusively by authorized therapy staff.

5. Cookies & Site Analytics

Our website uses basic functional cookies to maintain your session preferences (such as self-care quiz selections). You may choose to disable cookies in your browser settings at any time without affecting your ability to browse our therapy offerings.

6. Contact Privacy Officer

If you have any questions regarding our Privacy Policy or wish to request the deletion of your booking records, please contact our privacy representative:
